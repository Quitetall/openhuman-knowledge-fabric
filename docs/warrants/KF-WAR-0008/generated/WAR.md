# Intent

The owner uses the hosted Knowledge Fabric as its first real user, for real work, and checks that
it is correct and usable; everything he finds becomes recorded work, fixed under this Warrant or
moved to a later one by name.

This is milestone **M7, Owner correctness and user pass**. It is **run by the owner**. Agents do
not run the pass and do not judge it; they turn each finding into a fix, a test and a record.

## Why a person and not a gate

The two things this pass is for cannot be produced by a gate in this repository: whether the
product does what the owner meant (the twelve decisions of `docs/ROADMAP.md`), and the evidence
that needs a human on the real system: a person receiving an alert, a login against the real
realm, and the two latency bars of SAS §100.18 that include a person (recording an observation from
intent to durable, and attaching evidence).

## What is deliberately not in scope

- Commissioning (KF-WAR-0001). The pass starts on a commissioned host.
- v1.0 (KF-WAR-0009). The pass is its input.

# Basis

- **The host commissioned by KF-WAR-0001** (ADR 0039: a VPS on the tailnet, SeaweedFS, B2, the CPU
  embedder, LAMU `26923afb`), and the product built by KF-WAR-0004 to KF-WAR-0007.
- **ADR 0040** (to be written by KF-WAR-0003): the decisions the pass checks the product against.
- **ADR 0024** and SAS §100.18: two latency bars are unmeasured because each includes a person,
  and every recorded run is a workstation's. This pass is where both change.
- **ADR 0004**: a person receiving an alert and real-provider browser evidence are the two
  blockers no automation can close; KF-WAR-0001 carries them as RR-003.
- KF-SAS-RQ-171: claims needing host, provider or human evidence are outstanding until it exists.

# Work order

## The owner's pass (owner-only)

1. Sign in through the real realm from a phone and a laptop on the tailnet.
2. Use the product for real work for an agreed period (the owner chooses it): capture, search,
   ask the chat, verify agent submissions from Needs you, read the master document, invite one
   person through the M5 flow if ready.
3. Receive a real alert and a real Needs-you push on the phone, and say so.
4. Time the two person-included bars of §100.18: intent to durable for an observation, and
   attaching evidence.
5. Record each finding as an observation in KF itself, tagged for this Warrant, in his own words.

## The agents' work

1. Turn each finding into one of: a fix with a test that fails before it (in this Warrant); a new
   Warrant named in the finding (for anything larger than a fix); or a recorded "works as decided"
   with the decision it rests on.
2. Run `scripts/latency-bars.mjs` on the host and record every bar's figure from the host, closing
   the "every recorded run is a workstation's" part of §100.18.
3. Keep the finding list current in this Warrant's evidence, each finding with its disposition.
4. Propose the SAS changes the findings imply (§100.18 narrowed or closed, new §100 entries for
   anything accepted as a limit).

## Owner-only

Everything in "The owner's pass", the judgment that the pass is complete, authorizing and resolving
this Warrant.

# Assurance

## Acceptance obligations

### OBL-001 — the human evidence exists, from the host
- **scope:** KF-SAS-RQ-049, RQ-164, RQ-171; ADR 0004's two blockers no automation can close.
- **evidence:** the owner's own statement, recorded as an observation in KF, that he signed in
  through the real realm and received an alert and a push on his phone, with the date. A test
  cannot substitute for it and none is offered.

### OBL-002 — every bar has a host figure
- **scope:** KF-SAS-RQ-201; SAS §100.18.
- **checks:** `scripts/latency-bars.mjs` run on the host.
- **evidence:** every ADR 0024 bar with a figure measured on the host, including the two the owner
  timed; §100.18 narrowed or closed in the next SAS proposal.

### OBL-003 — no finding is lost
- **scope:** every finding the owner records against this Warrant during the pass.
- **evidence:** the count of findings recorded equals the count dispositioned (fixed with a test,
  moved to a named Warrant, or recorded as decided), counted from KF, not from a summary.
- **falsification:** a fix's test must fail on the code before the fix; a fix without such a test
  is recorded as unverified.

## Residual risk

**RR-001 — one user.** The pass is one person's use. It finds what the owner does; it says nothing
about what a second person would trip on, which is what M5's first invitees are for.
