# Intent

Make scope the product: what a person sees is what they have been granted, a role is a reusable
preset of that, and the person's master document is simply their scope compiled.

This is milestone **M3, Scope is the product**. In the owner's words (Q16): "A role is a preset on
their scope. An engineer gets all engineering documents … CEO gets CEO role and now has CEO scoped
view, so their master document contains everything a CEO has. Roles can contain other roles and be
subsets or supersets." And (Q9): the living org overview sits above the personal master record;
"I can give someone 0 access and they don't get a living org overview, but still get a master
record, or I can make a living org overview and include that person in it, where their master
record will include the living org overview."

## Deliverables, in one line each

1. Roles as composable scope presets, projected into the one grant view.
2. An access explanation that shows the role path.
3. The living org overview as a generated record that is in a reader's scope or is not.
4. The master-document page: the reader's scope compiled, readable as a calm document.
5. One dashboard for everyone, scoped by grants: Overview and master document, Needs you, Work in
   flight, Recent record, People and qualification; empty panels collapse.
6. Calm typography with a density switch to "extremely compact" (Q14).
7. Phone: read, verify, capture (Q12).
8. The two master-record read costs of SAS §100.42 and §100.43, so the master document is usable
   at the size of an organization.

## What is deliberately not in scope

- The in-app chat (M4). The dashboard leaves room for it; KF-WAR-0006 fills it.
- Start Here and qualification (M5). The People and qualification panel shows assignments and
  role membership until KF-WAR-0007 gives it stages.
