# Employee Handbook

**Effective:** January 2026
**Applies to:** All Munder Diffin Paper Shredding Co. employees

This handbook summarizes conduct expectations, working hours, time off,
confidentiality obligations, and reporting lines. It supplements, but does
not replace, your offer letter and any signed policy acknowledgments.

## Reporting Lines

Munder Diffin's leadership structure is intentionally flat:

- **Jim Miller** — Chief Executive Officer & Co-Founder (final authority
  on company policy, ownership matters, and strategic decisions)
- **Hank Shredder** — Co-Owner & Chief Operating Officer (day-to-day
  operations, reports to no one but the ownership vote of himself and Jim)
- **Dwight Blunt** — Operations Manager (fleet, routes, plant floor;
  reports to Hank Shredder)
- **Toby Flint** — HR & Compliance (policy administration, incident
  intake, employee relations; reports to Jim Miller)
- **Karen Filo** — Finance Approver / Controller (billing, payroll,
  spending approvals; reports to Jim Miller)
- **Pam Bealey** — Customer Success (accounts, onboarding, scheduling
  support; reports to Dwight Blunt)

Route drivers, plant floor staff, and contract personnel report to Dwight
Blunt or his designated shift lead.

## Working Hours

- Standard office hours are 8:00 AM–5:00 PM, Monday through Friday.
- Route crews and plant staff work scheduled shifts posted at least one
  week in advance by Dwight Blunt's team; routes typically run Monday
  through Saturday.
- Non-exempt employees must record time worked via the timekeeping system
  each shift; falsifying time records is grounds for termination.

## Paid Time Off (PTO)

| Tenure    | Annual PTO Accrual |
| --------- | ------------------ |
| 0–2 years | 10 days            |
| 2–5 years | 15 days            |
| 5+ years  | 20 days            |

PTO requests should be submitted at least 5 business days in advance
through your supervisor. Route and plant staff should coordinate with
Dwight Blunt to ensure adequate crew coverage. Unused PTO carries over up
to a maximum of 5 days into the next calendar year.

## Standards of Conduct

- Treat customers, coworkers, and the public with professionalism and
  respect at all times, especially while representing the company on-site
  at a customer location.
- Uniforms and photo badges must be worn during all customer-facing
  shifts.
- Company vehicles are for business use only; personal use requires
  written approval from Dwight Blunt.
- Any conflict of interest (e.g., a financial relationship with a vendor
  or competitor) must be disclosed to Toby Flint.

## Confidentiality Obligation

Because our business is built on customers trusting us with their most
sensitive materials, every employee — regardless of role — signs a
confidentiality agreement at hire. Key obligations:

- Never view, copy, photograph, or discuss the contents of any material
  collected for destruction.
- Do not remove customer materials from a truck, bin, or plant floor for
  any purpose other than scheduled destruction.
- Report any suspected breach of custody immediately to your supervisor
  and to Toby Flint, regardless of how minor it may seem.
- Confidentiality obligations survive termination of employment.

## Workplace Safety

Route and plant staff must complete safety training before operating
shredding equipment and must wear required personal protective equipment
(gloves, eye protection, high-visibility vests) at all times on the plant
floor or during on-site collection.

## Reporting Concerns

Employees may raise workplace concerns, safety issues, or policy
questions with their direct supervisor or with Toby Flint
(toby.flint@munderdiffin.example) at any time. Concerns raised in good
faith will not result in retaliation.

## Acknowledgment

All employees are required to sign an acknowledgment of this handbook
within their first week of employment; a copy is retained by Toby Flint's
office.
