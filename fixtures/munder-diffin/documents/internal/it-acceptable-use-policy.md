# IT Acceptable Use Policy

**Owner:** Toby Flint, HR & Compliance
**Applies to:** All employees and contractors with access to Munder
Diffin systems, including the records platform, email, and route devices

This policy governs use of company-owned devices, accounts, and the
records platform that stores custody logs, certificates of destruction,
and customer account information.

## Devices

- Company-issued laptops, route tablets, and phones are for business use.
  Limited incidental personal use (e.g., checking a personal email during
  a break) is permitted but must not interfere with work or expose
  company systems to risk.
- Devices must not be left unlocked and unattended in a vehicle, customer
  site, or public location.
- Lost or stolen devices must be reported to Toby Flint's office
  **immediately**, so access can be revoked and the device remotely
  wiped where supported.
- Only IT-approved software may be installed on company devices; personal
  software installs require written approval.

## Passwords and Account Access

- All accounts on the records platform, email, and dispatch system
  require a unique password of at least 12 characters; shared logins are
  not permitted under any circumstance.
- Multi-factor authentication is required for the records platform and
  for any account with access to customer data, pricing, or partner
  agreements.
- Passwords must not be written down, shared verbally, or stored in an
  unencrypted document.
- Suspected account compromise must be reported to Toby Flint the same
  day; the affected account will be suspended pending review.

## The Records Platform

- Access to the records platform is role-based: route and plant staff see
  only the job-level custody logging module; Customer Success sees
  account and scheduling records; Finance sees billing and invoicing
  records; executives and Finance Approver Karen Filo see pricing and
  margin data.
- Do not export customer data, custody logs, or certificates from the
  platform to a personal device, personal email, or removable media.
- Do not share your platform login credentials with a contractor,
  temporary worker, or another employee, even to help them complete a
  task; request a proper access grant from Toby Flint instead.
- Access is reviewed quarterly; access no longer needed for an employee's
  or contractor's current role is removed automatically.

## Email and Communication

- Company email (@munderdiffin.example) is the only approved channel for
  sending certificates of destruction, invoices, or any customer-specific
  document.
- Do not forward customer data or internal pricing information to
  personal email accounts.
- Marketing and customer communications must be approved through the
  Customer Success or Operations lead before sending to a broad customer
  list.

## Prohibited Use

The following are prohibited on any company device or account:

- Accessing, copying, or photographing the contents of any material
  collected for destruction, using any device.
- Installing unlicensed or unapproved software, including remote-access
  tools not provisioned by IT.
- Using company systems to access or store material unrelated to Munder
  Diffin business.
- Disabling or bypassing security controls (antivirus, device encryption,
  multi-factor authentication) on any company device.
- Sharing internal pricing, margin, partner, or board-level documents
  outside their designated access group.

## Reporting Issues

Report any suspected security incident, lost device, phishing attempt, or
policy question to Toby Flint (toby.flint@munderdiffin.example). Timely
reporting is not a disciplinary matter; failing to report a known issue
is.

## Enforcement

Violations of this policy may result in disciplinary action up to and
including termination of employment or contract, consistent with the
Employee Handbook and Contractor Engagement Guide.
