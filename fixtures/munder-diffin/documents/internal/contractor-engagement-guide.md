# Contractor Engagement Guide

**Owner:** Toby Flint, HR & Compliance / Dwight Blunt, Operations
**Applies to:** Contract drivers and temporary plant labor (e.g., Ryan Temp
and other agency or 1099 personnel)

Munder Diffin occasionally engages contract drivers and temporary plant
staff to cover route volume spikes and seasonal demand. Because
contractors handle the same sensitive material as employees, they are
held to the same custody standards, with some access restrictions that
apply specifically to non-employee personnel.

## Before Your First Shift

Every contractor must complete the following before being assigned a
route or plant shift:

1. Sign the Munder Diffin Contractor Confidentiality Agreement (separate
   from, but equivalent in scope to, the employee confidentiality
   agreement).
2. Pass a background check administered through Toby Flint's office.
3. Complete the half-day custody and safety orientation covering the
   Chain-of-Custody Procedure and basic shred-truck safety.
4. Be issued a temporary photo badge, clearly marked "CONTRACTOR," valid
   for the duration of the engagement.

## Badge Requirements

- Your contractor badge must be worn, visible, at all times while on a
  customer site, in a company vehicle, or on the plant floor.
- Badges are non-transferable and must be returned to Dwight Blunt's
  office at the end of each engagement or contract renewal period,
  whichever comes first.
- A lost badge must be reported the same day; a replacement is issued
  only after Toby Flint confirms the loss report.

## Vehicle Checks

Contract drivers operating a Munder Diffin shred truck or collection
vehicle must:

- Complete the same pre-trip inspection checklist required of employee
  drivers (see Shredding Operations SOP §1), including shredder blade
  condition, hydraulic fluid, and bin seal integrity.
- Report any mechanical issue to dispatch before departing the yard.
- Never operate a vehicle without a valid, current driver's license on
  file with Dwight Blunt's office.

## What Contractors May Access

Contractors may:

- Operate assigned shred trucks and collection vehicles.
- Collect, log, seal, and transport customer materials per the
  Chain-of-Custody Procedure.
- View and complete custody logs for jobs they perform.

## What Contractors May NOT Access

Contractors may **not**:

- Access the records platform's customer account records, billing
  history, or pricing data (system access is limited to the job-level
  custody logging module).
- View or handle materials or job manifests for jobs they are not
  assigned to.
- Access the confidential pricing and margin sheet, partner agreements,
  or any board or ownership-level records.
- Represent themselves as an employee of Munder Diffin in any customer
  communication; contractors identify themselves as "working with Munder
  Diffin" when asked.
- Take on a second Munder Diffin contract role without disclosure to
  Toby Flint (conflict-of-interest check).

## Two-Person Rule Applies

Contract drivers assigned to hard drive or media pickups are subject to
the same two-person verification rule as employees, per the
Chain-of-Custody Procedure §3. A contractor may serve as one of the two
required verifiers but never as the sole verifying party.

## Ending an Engagement

At the end of an engagement, the contractor's badge, vehicle fob (if
issued), and any company equipment must be returned to Dwight Blunt's
office. System access is revoked by IT the same business day the
engagement ends, per notice from Toby Flint.

## Questions

Contractors with questions about scope, badge issues, or access should
contact Dwight Blunt (operations) or Toby Flint (compliance and HR
matters) directly rather than a site contact or customer.
