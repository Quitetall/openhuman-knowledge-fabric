# Standard Operating Procedure: Mobile Shred Job

**Owner:** Dwight Blunt, Operations Manager
**Applies to:** All route drivers and shred truck operators
**Last reviewed:** March 2026

This SOP defines the required steps for a mobile (on-site) shredding job,
from pre-trip inspection through certificate issuance. Deviations must be
logged as exceptions and reported to Dwight Blunt the same day.

## 1. Pre-Trip

1. Inspect the shred truck: verify shredder blade condition, hydraulic
   fluid level, tarp/seal integrity on the collection bin, and that the
   on-board camera (for customer viewing) is functional.
2. Confirm the day's route manifest against the dispatch system, including
   job numbers, addresses, and expected container counts.
3. Verify you have sufficient empty consoles or replacement bins for any
   stops requiring a swap.
4. Confirm your photo badge and uniform are on your person before leaving
   the yard.

## 2. Arrival and Check-In

1. Check in with the customer's site contact (front desk, dock manager,
   or the account contact on file).
2. Present your Munder Diffin photo badge if requested; this is required
   at every stop, not only on request.
3. Position the truck for safe access to the loading area or console
   location.

## 3. Bin Collection

1. Locate each locked console/bin at the site using the container ID on
   the manifest.
2. Verify the container ID sticker matches the manifest entry before
   collection.
3. Weigh or estimate volume per company procedure and record it.
4. For hard drives or media collected in a separate secure bin, follow
   the two-person verification step in the Chain-of-Custody Procedure
   before loading.

## 4. Custody Log

1. Record on the paper or digital custody log: job number, container ID,
   time of pickup, approximate weight, and driver initials.
2. Where the site contact is available, obtain their signature or
   initials on the log as pickup confirmation.
3. The custody log travels with the load until destruction is complete
   and is never left unattended with the customer's materials.

## 5. Shred

1. For on-site shredding, position the truck at the collection point and
   load material directly into the shred chamber; invite the customer to
   observe via the viewing window if present.
2. Shred to the cross-cut/particle specification defined for the
   customer's service tier.
3. If on-site shredding is not being performed (plant-bound job), seal
   each container with a tamper-evident lock before it leaves the
   customer's premises and note the seal number on the custody log.

## 6. Certificate Issuance

1. Upon confirmed destruction (on-site, same visit) or upon plant
   processing (typically same business day), generate the Certificate of
   Destruction referencing the job number, date, material description,
   approximate weight, and destruction method.
2. Certificates are transmitted to the customer within 2 business days
   and archived in the records platform for a minimum of three years.

## 7. Exceptions

Log an exception and notify Dwight Blunt immediately if any of the
following occurs:

- A container's ID does not match the manifest.
- A seal is found broken or missing on arrival at the plant.
- Access to the site is denied or the site contact is unavailable after
  two check-in attempts.
- The shred truck experiences a mechanical failure mid-route requiring a
  material transfer to a second vehicle.
- Any material is left unattended outside the truck or a locked area for
  any period of time.

Exceptions are reviewed weekly by Operations and, where they touch
custody integrity, escalated to Toby Flint for compliance review.
