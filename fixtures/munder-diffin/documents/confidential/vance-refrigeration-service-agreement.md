# Vance Refrigeration Service Agreement — Summary

**Classification:** Confidential — Executive, Finance, and Vance
Refrigeration (for their own agreement)
**Customer contact:** Bob Vance, Vance Refrigeration
(bob.vance@vancerefrigeration.example)
**Account owner:** Pam Bealey, Customer Success | **Approved by:** Karen Filo
**Term:** March 1, 2026 – February 28, 2027 (12 months, auto-renew)

## Overview

This document summarizes the negotiated service agreement between Munder
Diffin Paper Shredding Co. and Vance Refrigeration for recurring document
and hard drive destruction across three sites. It is a summary for
internal reference; the fully executed contract on file with Karen Filo's
office governs in the event of any discrepancy.

## Sites Covered

| Site                                          | Address (short)          | Service Type                                               |
| --------------------------------------------- | ------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| Vance Refrigeration — HQ & Sales Office       | Main St. location        | Scheduled Route (paper)                                    |
| Vance Refrigeration — Distribution Center     | Industrial Pkwy location | Scheduled Route (paper) + quarterly Hard Drive Destruction |
| Vance Refrigeration — Service Dispatch Office | Route 9 location         | Scheduled Route (paper)                                    |

## Pickup Cadence

- **HQ & Sales Office:** Biweekly pickup, two 32-gallon locked consoles.
- **Distribution Center:** Weekly pickup, three 64-gallon locked consoles
  (higher volume due to shipping and invoicing paperwork); quarterly
  on-site Hard Drive Destruction for retired dispatch terminals.
- **Service Dispatch Office:** Monthly pickup, one 32-gallon console.

## Term and Renewal

- Initial term: 12 months from March 1, 2026.
- Automatically renews for successive 12-month terms unless either party
  provides 60 days' written notice before the renewal date.
- Pricing is fixed for the initial term; any renewal-term price change is
  proposed no later than 45 days before renewal and requires Karen Filo's
  sign-off before it is communicated to Bob Vance.

## Service Commitments

- Pickup windows are confirmed with the site contact at each Vance
  Refrigeration location at account setup; Pam Bealey's team manages
  scheduling changes.
- Certificates of Destruction are issued per site, per pickup, and
  emailed to Bob Vance and the relevant site contact within 2 business
  days.
- Hard Drive Destruction events at the Distribution Center follow the
  two-person verification rule per the Chain-of-Custody Procedure, given
  the sensitivity of dispatch and routing data on retired terminals.

## Account History Notes

- Vance Refrigeration has been a Munder Diffin customer since the
  Distribution Center site was added to the Scheduled Route program in
  2024; the HQ and Service Dispatch sites were added under this current
  agreement in March 2026.
- No missed-pickup exceptions have been logged for this account in the
  trailing 12 months as of this summary's writing.
