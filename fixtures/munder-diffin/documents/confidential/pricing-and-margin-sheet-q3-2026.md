## Volume Discount Bands

| Monthly Volume (est.)                    | Standard Discount | Approval Required                |
| ---------------------------------------- | ----------------- | -------------------------------- |
| Under 500 lb                             | 0%                | None                             |
| 500–1,500 lb                             | Up to 8%          | Dwight Blunt (Operations)        |
| 1,500–5,000 lb                           | Up to 15%         | Dwight Blunt + Karen Filo        |
| Over 5,000 lb, or any discount above 15% | Case-by-case      | **Karen Filo approval required** |

**Discount authority:** Any discount exceeding 15% off list price, on any
service or contract term, requires written approval from **Karen Filo**
before the quote is issued to the customer, regardless of deal size or
customer tenure. Sales and Operations staff may not commit to a discount
above this threshold verbally or in a draft proposal pending her sign-off.

## Partner Referral Pricing

Referral partners (see Sabre Printers Partner Integration Spec) receive a
wholesale rate approximately 18% below standard list price on referred
accounts, with the referral fee paid from the resulting margin rather than
added to customer pricing. Wholesale rate changes require Karen Filo's
approval and a corresponding update to the partner agreement.

## Q3 2026 Target Margin

Company-wide blended gross margin target for Q3 2026 is **58%**, up from
55.8% in Q2, driven by route density improvements in the eastern service
area and a modest price adjustment on Scheduled Route mid-size accounts
effective August 1, 2026.
