# Munder Diffin Paper Shredding Co. — Service Overview

Munder Diffin Paper Shredding Co. is a regional secure document destruction
company serving businesses, healthcare practices, law firms, and government
offices. We provide on-site mobile shredding, off-site plant shredding, and
certified hard-drive and media destruction, backed by a documented chain of
custody at every step.

## Who We Serve

Since our founding, Munder Diffin has grown to serve small offices through
multi-site enterprise accounts across the tri-county service area, with
routes running Monday through Saturday. Our customer base spans medical
clinics, accounting firms, insurance brokers, school districts, and
manufacturing companies with regulated records (HR files, financial
statements, patient intake forms, and design documents).

## Coverage Area

Our mobile shred trucks and pickup vans currently cover the metro service
area and surrounding counties within roughly a 60-mile radius of our
processing plant. Customers outside that radius are served via scheduled
plant routes; contact our office to confirm coverage for a specific address.

## How a Job Works, End to End

1. **Request a quote.** A customer submits a request through our website,
   phone line, or an account representative. Pam Bealey's Customer Success
   team confirms volume, material type (paper, hard drives, mixed media),
   and preferred cadence.
2. **Scheduling.** The job is placed on the route calendar. Recurring
   accounts receive a standing pickup window; one-time jobs are scheduled
   within the stated turnaround.
3. **On-site or drop-off collection.** A uniformed, badged driver arrives
   with a locked shred truck (for on-site shredding) or a collection
   vehicle (for plant transport). Locked consoles and bins are weighed or
   counted and logged.
4. **Custody log.** Every container is recorded on a custody log at the
   point of pickup, including container ID, approximate weight, and time.
5. **Destruction.** Materials are either shredded on-site to
   cross-cut/particle specification while the customer can observe via the
   truck's camera window, or transported under seal to the plant for
   processing that business day.
6. **Certificate of Destruction.** Once destruction is confirmed, we issue
   a signed Certificate of Destruction referencing the job number, date,
   material description, and destruction method, suitable for compliance
   and audit files.
7. **Recycling.** Shredded paper is baled and sent to a licensed recycling
   partner; destroyed media is separated for e-waste recycling.

## Compliance Standard

Munder Diffin's operations follow a NAID AAA–style chain-of-custody model:
background-checked and badged personnel, locked collection containers,
tamper-evident seals for off-site transport, and destruction logs retained
for a minimum of three years.
