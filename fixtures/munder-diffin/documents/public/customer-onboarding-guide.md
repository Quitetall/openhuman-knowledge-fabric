# Customer Onboarding Guide

Welcome to Munder Diffin Paper Shredding Co. This guide walks new customers
through account setup, the first pickup, and what to expect from your
ongoing service relationship.

## Step 1: Account Setup

After you request a quote, a member of our Customer Success team — your
primary contact is **Pam Bealey** (pam.bealey@munderdiffin.example,
555-0142) — will confirm the following details with you:

- Service address(es) and access instructions (loading dock, suite number,
  gate codes)
- Material type: office paper, mixed media, hard drives, or a combination
- Estimated monthly volume (number of standard consoles or banker's boxes)
- Preferred service tier: On-Demand Purge, Scheduled Route, or Hard Drive
  & Media Destruction
- Billing contact and preferred invoicing cadence (monthly or per-service)

Once confirmed, Pam's team issues a Customer Account Number and, for
scheduled accounts, places your first pickup on the route calendar,
typically within 5–7 business days of signup.

## Step 2: Container Placement

If your service includes locked consoles or bins, our team delivers and
places them at your site before or during the first pickup. Standard
console sizes are 32-gallon (for general office paper) and 64-gallon (for
higher-volume areas). Each container ships with a unique container ID
sticker used for custody tracking.

## Step 3: What to Expect on the First Pickup

1. Your assigned driver arrives within the scheduled two-hour window and
   checks in at the front desk or dock, presenting a Munder Diffin photo
   badge.
2. The driver logs each container's ID and approximate weight on the
   custody log, which your site contact may review and sign.
3. For on-site shredding, the truck's shred chamber is parked at the
   service door; customers are welcome to observe destruction through the
   truck's viewing window.
4. For plant (off-site) shredding, containers are sealed with
   tamper-evident locks before leaving your site and transported directly
   to our secure processing plant.
5. The driver leaves a pickup receipt noting the date, container count,
   and job number.

## Step 4: Certificate of Destruction

Within 2 business days of destruction, you will receive a **Certificate of
Destruction** by email, referencing:

- Job number and service date
- Material description and approximate weight or volume
- Destruction method (on-site cross-cut shred or plant particle shred)
- Signature of the certifying operations lead

Retain these certificates for your own compliance and audit records; we
also retain a copy for a minimum of three years per our chain-of-custody
policy.

## Step 5: Ongoing Service

For scheduled accounts, pickups recur automatically on your chosen
cadence. You can adjust frequency, add a location, or pause service by
contacting Customer Success at least 5 business days before your next
scheduled pickup.
