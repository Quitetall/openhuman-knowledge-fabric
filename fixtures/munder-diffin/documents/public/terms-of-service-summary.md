# Terms of Service — Plain-Language Summary

This page summarizes the key points of Munder Diffin Paper Shredding Co.'s
Terms of Service in plain language. It is not a substitute for the full
signed service agreement, which governs in the event of any conflict.

## Scheduling and Access

- Scheduled Route customers receive a recurring pickup window (weekly,
  biweekly, or monthly, as agreed at signup).
- Customers must provide reasonable access to collection containers during
  the scheduled window. If our driver cannot access containers after two
  consecutive attempts, the pickup is marked missed and rescheduled at the
  customer's request.
- On-Demand Purge jobs are scheduled for a specific date; we ask for at
  least 48 hours' notice to reschedule.

## Cancellation

- Scheduled Route service may be canceled by either party with 30 days'
  written notice.
- On-Demand Purge and Hard Drive & Media Destruction jobs canceled with
  less than 24 hours' notice before the scheduled service window may incur
  a trip charge of up to $75, to cover truck and crew dispatch.
- Leased locked consoles remain Munder Diffin property and must be made
  available for pickup within 10 business days of service cancellation.

## What Happens If Something Goes Wrong

- Munder Diffin carries commercial general liability and professional
  (errors & omissions) insurance covering our destruction operations.
- Our liability for any single incident related to a service failure is
  limited to the fees paid for the specific service in question over the
  preceding 12 months, except where such limitation is not permitted by
  law.
- We are not liable for indirect, incidental, or consequential damages
  (such as lost business) arising from a delayed or missed pickup, except
  in cases of gross negligence or willful misconduct.
- Any claim related to a service must be submitted in writing within 60
  days of the service date.

## Data Destruction Standard

- All destruction services follow a NAID AAA–style chain-of-custody
  standard: badged and background-checked personnel, container-level
  custody logging, tamper-evident seals for off-site transport, and
  destruction confirmed same-day for on-site shredding.
- Paper destroyed on-site is cut to a cross-cut/particle specification
  consistent with commonly recognized secure-destruction guidance; paper
  destroyed at our plant is processed to an equivalent or finer
  specification.
- Hard drives and electronic media are physically destroyed (shredded or
  crushed) such that data recovery by standard forensic means is not
  feasible.
- We issue a Certificate of Destruction for every completed job, which
  serves as the customer's record of compliant destruction.

## Confidentiality

- Munder Diffin personnel and contractors handling customer materials are
  bound by confidentiality obligations and do not review, copy, or retain
  the contents of any material collected for destruction.

## Payment Terms

- Invoices for Scheduled Route accounts are issued monthly, net 15.
- On-Demand and Hard Drive & Media Destruction jobs may be invoiced per
  service or included in the next monthly statement, per the customer's
  billing preference.
- Accounts more than 30 days past due may have service suspended until
  balance is resolved.
