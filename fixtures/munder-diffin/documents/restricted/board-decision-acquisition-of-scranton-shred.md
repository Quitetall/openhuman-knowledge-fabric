# Decision Record: Acquisition of Scranton Shred LLC

**Classification:** Restricted — Owners Only (Jim Miller, Hank Shredder)
**Decision date:** August 14, 2026
**Recorded by:** Jim Miller

## Background

Scranton Shred LLC is a smaller competitor operating in the northern
portion of our service area, running two mobile shred trucks and a single
leased plant facility. Scranton Shred's owner approached Hank Shredder in
June 2026 about a possible sale, citing succession planning and rising
plant lease costs. This record documents the decision to pursue
acquisition, the options considered, the financial basis, and the vote.

## Options Considered

1. **Acquire Scranton Shred LLC outright** — purchase the customer book,
   two trucks, and assume or renegotiate the plant lease; wind down the
   Scranton Shred brand into Munder Diffin within 6 months.
2. **Acquire the customer book only** — purchase the ~140-account
   customer list without the trucks or lease, and service new accounts
   from existing Munder Diffin routes and plant capacity.
3. **Decline and compete organically** — continue building density in the
   northern service area without acquiring Scranton Shred, accepting
   slower share gain but no integration risk.
4. **Wait and revisit** — defer any decision 6–12 months to see whether
   Scranton Shred's asking price softens or another buyer emerges.

## Financial Summary

| Item                                            | Figure                             |
| ----------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| Scranton Shred asking price (full acquisition)  | $410,000                           |
| Estimated annualized revenue acquired           | $520,000                           |
| Estimated annual route consolidation savings    | $65,000/year                       |
| Truck refurbishment/integration cost (2 trucks) | $38,000 (one-time)                 |
| Plant lease assumption (18 months remaining)    | $9,200/month                       |
| Projected payback period (full acquisition)     | ~2.4 years                         |
| Customer-book-only alternative price            | $265,000                           |
| Customer-book-only projected payback            | ~3.1 years (no truck/lease upside) |

Karen Filo prepared the underlying financial model and confirmed the
2.4-year payback for the full acquisition option assumes retention of at
least 80% of Scranton Shred's existing customer accounts through the
first 12 months post-close.

## Discussion

Hank Shredder favored full acquisition (Option 1), citing the immediate
route density gain in the northern territory and the below-market truck
valuation implied in the asking price. Jim Miller raised integration risk
— specifically, whether Scranton Shred's existing custody logging
practices meet our chain-of-custody standard — and required that a
custody-practice audit be completed before close, regardless of which
option is chosen.

Option 3 (decline) was discussed but set aside given the payback period
and the risk of a competing buyer consolidating the northern territory
instead.

## Vote

| Owner         | Vote    | Option                                             |
| ------------- | ------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| Jim Miller    | Approve | Option 1 (full acquisition), conditioned per below |
| Hank Shredder | Approve | Option 1 (full acquisition)                        |

**Result: Approved 2–0.** Munder Diffin will pursue full acquisition of
Scranton Shred LLC.

## Conditions of Approval

1. A custody-practice audit of Scranton Shred's operations, led by Toby
   Flint, must be completed and findings remediated (or a remediation
   plan agreed) before close.
2. Final purchase price is capped at $410,000 pending confirmatory
   diligence on the plant lease terms.
3. Karen Filo must confirm financing does not require drawing down more
   than 40% of current cash reserves.
4. Dwight Blunt is to prepare a route integration plan assuming a 6-month
   wind-down of the Scranton Shred brand.
